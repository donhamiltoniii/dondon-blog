## Chocolate Raspberry Mousse

_Prep 5 min · Cook 0 min · Serves 1_

Thick, whipped Greek yogurt with cocoa and thawed raspberries folded through. Tastes like a chocolate-covered raspberry.

### Ingredients

- Nonfat Greek yogurt (250g)
- Unsweetened cocoa powder (1 tbsp)
- Frozen raspberries, thawed (100g)
- Vanilla extract (1/2 tsp)
- Zero-calorie sweetener (to taste)

### Instructions

1. Whisk the yogurt, cocoa, vanilla, and sweetener for a full minute, until smooth and slightly fluffy.
2. Fold in the thawed raspberries, leaving some whole and mashing some into the yogurt.
3. Eat right away, or chill for 20 minutes for a firmer texture.

## Black Bean Brownie Bites

_Prep 5 min · Cook 18 min · Serves 1_

Blender brownie bites made from black beans, whey, and cocoa. Makes four mini muffin-size bites.

### Ingredients

- Canned black beans, drained and rinsed (125g)
- Vanilla whey protein (1 scoop (30g))
- Egg (1 large)
- Unsweetened cocoa powder (2 tbsp)
- Baking powder (1/2 tsp)
- Vanilla extract (1/2 tsp)
- Zero-calorie sweetener (to taste)

### Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease four muffin cups.
2. Blend everything until completely smooth, adding a tablespoon or two of water only if the batter is too thick to pour.
3. Divide the batter among the muffin cups and bake for 15-18 minutes, until set in the center.
4. Cool for 5 minutes. They firm up as they cool.

## Pumpkin Pie Yogurt

_Prep 5 min · Cook 0 min · Serves 1_

Greek yogurt, canned pumpkin, and chia with pumpkin pie spice. Tastes like the filling without the crust.

### Ingredients

- Nonfat Greek yogurt (200g)
- Canned pumpkin puree (120g)
- Chia seeds (1 tbsp)
- Pumpkin pie spice (1 tsp)
- Zero-calorie sweetener (to taste)

### Instructions

1. Stir the pumpkin, pie spice, and sweetener into the yogurt until evenly combined.
2. Stir in the chia seeds.
3. Let it sit for 10 minutes so the chia thickens it, or refrigerate overnight.

## Chickpea Cookie Dough

_Prep 5 min · Cook 0 min · Serves 1_

Blended chickpeas, whey, and powdered peanut butter with a few chocolate chips. Eat it with a spoon.

### Ingredients

- Canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed (130g)
- Vanilla whey protein (1 scoop (30g))
- Powdered peanut butter (2 tbsp (12g))
- Whole milk (2 tbsp (30ml))
- Vanilla extract (1/2 tsp)
- Dark chocolate chips (1 tbsp (12g))

### Instructions

1. Blend the chickpeas, whey, powdered peanut butter, milk, and vanilla in a food processor until a thick, smooth dough forms, scraping down the sides as needed.

## PB Protein Chia Pudding

_Prep 5 min · Cook 0 min · Serves 1_

Make-ahead chia pudding with whey and powdered peanut butter whisked into the milk.

### Ingredients

- Whole milk (180ml)
- Vanilla whey protein (1 scoop (30g))
- Powdered peanut butter (2 tbsp (12g))
- Chia seeds (2 tbsp)
- Vanilla extract (1/2 tsp)

### Instructions

1. Whisk the whey and powdered peanut butter into the milk first so they don't clump.
2. Add the chia seeds and vanilla and whisk again.
3. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours, ideally overnight, stirring once after 10 minutes to prevent clumping.

## Banana PB Nice Cream

_Prep 5 min · Cook 0 min · Serves 1_

Frozen banana blended with whey and powdered peanut butter into thick soft serve.

### Ingredients

- Banana, frozen and sliced (1 medium (118g))
- Vanilla whey protein (1 scoop (30g))

1. Add the milk to a blender first, then the banana, whey, powdered peanut butter, and chia.

## Cheesecake Cottage Cheese Bowl

_Prep 5 min · Cook 0 min · Serves 1_

Blended cottage cheese with lemon and vanilla, topped with blueberries and a crushed bran cereal crumble.

- Vanilla extract (1/2 tsp)
