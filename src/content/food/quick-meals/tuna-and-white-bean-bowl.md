### Ingredients

- Canned light tuna in water, drained (2 cans, ~140g drained)
- Cannellini beans, canned, drained & rinsed (1 can, ~260g drained)
- Mixed greens (100g)
- Olive oil (14g / 1 tbsp)
- Lemon, juiced (1/2 lemon)
- Salt, black pepper, red pepper flakes (to taste)

### Instructions

1. Drain and flake the tuna into a bowl.
2. Drain and rinse the cannellini beans; add to the bowl.
3. Add the mixed greens.
4. Dress with olive oil and fresh lemon juice; season with salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes.
5. Toss to combine and serve.
