### Ingredients

- 225g Costco chargrilled chipotle chicken
- 1 rice cup (Kroger jasmine rice or Costco Bibigo sticky rice)
- ~90g salsa roja

### Instructions

1. Heat the rice cup according to package instructions.
2. Top with chicken and salsa roja.
