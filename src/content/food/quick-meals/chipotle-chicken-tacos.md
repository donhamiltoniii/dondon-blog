## Ingredients

- 3 Mission Carb Balance flour tortillas
- 228g Kirkland Flame Seared Chipotle Chicken
- 28g Kirkland cheddar jack cheese

## Instructions

1. Warm the tortillas until pliable.
2. Heat the chipotle chicken through (skillet or microwave).
3. Divide chicken across the three tortillas, top each with cheddar jack, and fold.
