## Greek Yogurt Tzatziki Chicken Bowl

_Prep 10 min · Cook 0 min · Serves 1_

No-cook bowl of shredded rotisserie chicken under a garlicky yogurt-cucumber sauce, with tomatoes and cucumber on the side.

### Ingredients

- Rotisserie chicken breast, shredded (140g)
- Nonfat Greek yogurt (170g)
- Cucumber, half grated and half sliced (150g)
- Cherry tomatoes (100g)
- Garlic, minced (1 clove)
- Fresh dill, chopped (2g)
- Lemon juice (1 tbsp)

### Instructions

1. Grate half the cucumber and squeeze out the extra water. Stir it into the yogurt with the garlic, dill, and lemon juice.
2. Pile the shredded chicken in a bowl and spoon the sauce over it.
3. Add the sliced cucumber and halved cherry tomatoes on the side.

## Egg Roll in a Bowl (Single Serving)

_Prep 5 min · Cook 10 min · Serves 1_

One-pan ground turkey and cabbage with ginger, garlic, and sriracha. The batch-prep version is in its own recipe file.

### Ingredients

- Ground turkey, 93% lean, raw (150g)
- Coleslaw mix (130g)
- Low-sodium soy sauce (1 tbsp)
- Garlic, minced (1 clove)
- Fresh ginger, grated (1 tsp)
- Sriracha (1.5 tsp)
- Toasted sesame oil (1 tsp)

### Instructions

1. Brown the turkey in a pan over medium-high heat for 5-6 minutes, breaking it up.
2. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add the coleslaw mix and soy sauce and cook for 3-4 minutes, until the cabbage wilts but still has some bite.
4. Off the heat, stir in the sriracha and sesame oil.

## White Bean and Tuna Salad

_Prep 5 min · Cook 0 min · Serves 1_

Canned cannellini beans and tuna with red onion, parsley, olive oil, and lemon.

### Ingredients

- Canned cannellini beans, drained and rinsed (1 cup (260g))
- Canned tuna in water, drained (1 can (112g))
- Red onion, thinly sliced (40g)
- Fresh parsley, chopped (5g)
- Olive oil (2 tsp)
- Lemon juice (2 tbsp)

### Instructions

1. Combine the beans, tuna, onion, and parsley in a bowl.
2. Dress with the olive oil and lemon juice, then toss gently.

## Berry Chia Pudding

_Prep 5 min · Cook 0 min · Serves 1_

Make-ahead chia pudding with milk, vanilla, and berries.

### Ingredients

- Chia seeds (2 tbsp)
- Whole milk (180ml)
- Vanilla extract (1/2 tsp)
- Frozen strawberries, thawed and mashed (100g)

### Instructions

1. Whisk the chia seeds, milk, and vanilla in a jar.
2. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours, ideally overnight, stirring once after 10 minutes to prevent clumping.
