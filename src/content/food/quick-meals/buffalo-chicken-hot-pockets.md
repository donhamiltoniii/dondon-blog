### Ingredients

**Dough**

- Self-rising flour (500g)
- Greek yogurt, 0% fat (520g)
- Garlic salt (15g)
- Italian seasoning (8g)

**Buffalo Chicken Filling**

- Chicken breast (1135g)
- Garlic salt (7g)
- Smoked paprika (3g)
- Ranch seasoning (8g)
- Black pepper (3g)
- Tomato salsa (60g)
- Diced white onion (100g)
- Buffalo sauce (120g)
- Fat-free mozzarella (392g)
- Fresh chives (10g)

**High-Protein Buffalo Sauce**

- Cottage cheese (335g)
- 1/3-fat cream cheese (56g)
- Garlic salt (7g)
- Black pepper (3g)
- Onion powder (3g)
- Buffalo sauce (120g)
- Fat-free milk (60g)

### Instructions

**High-Protein Buffalo Sauce**

1. Blend the cottage cheese, cream cheese, garlic salt, black pepper, onion powder, buffalo sauce, and fat-free milk until completely smooth. Set aside.

**Buffalo Chicken Filling**

1. Season the chicken breast with garlic salt, smoked paprika, ranch seasoning, and black pepper.
2. Cook the chicken through (bake, air fry, or pan-sear), then shred.
3. Combine the shredded chicken with the salsa, diced onion, buffalo sauce, high-protein buffalo sauce, mozzarella, and chives. Mix well.

**Dough**

1. Combine the self-rising flour, Greek yogurt, garlic salt, and Italian seasoning. Mix and knead into a cohesive dough.
2. Divide into 10 equal portions and roll each out flat.

**Assemble**

1. Add filling to each dough round, fold over, and seal the edges.
2. Bake at 400°F until golden, roughly 18–22 minutes.

**Freezing**

1. Wrap each hot pocket in aluminum foil or place in an airtight freezer bag.
2. Freeze for up to 1 month.

**Reheating**

1. Wrap a frozen hot pocket in a wet paper towel.
2. Microwave on a plate for 3–4 minutes.
3. Toast in a pan or air fry for a few minutes if you want it crispy.
