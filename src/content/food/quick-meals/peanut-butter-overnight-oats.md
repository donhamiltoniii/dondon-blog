### Ingredients

- One Degree sprouted rolled oats (300g)
- Whole milk (450ml)
- Greek yogurt, 2% fat (850g)
- Peanut butter powder (120g)
- Ascent 100% Whey, Vanilla Bean (5 rounded scoops, 155g)
- Honey (5 tbsp, 105g)
- Salt (5 pinches)
- Fruit of choice (per bowl, not included in macros)

### Instructions

1. Weigh your empty large bowl and write down the number. In it, combine the oats, peanut butter powder, whey, and salt.
2. Add the milk, Greek yogurt, and honey and stir until no dry pockets of powder remain.
3. Weigh the full bowl. Subtract the bowl's weight; the batch should be about 1,980g. Divide by 6 to get your portion (about 330g).
4. Weigh out each portion into 6 containers, cover, and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, or overnight.
5. Stir before eating, adding a splash of milk if it's too thick, and top with the fruit of your choice.
