### Ingredients

- 170g Kirkland nonfat plain Greek yogurt
- 1 serving (⅓ cup) Purely Elizabeth Chocolate Chip Cookie granola
- 100g mixed berries

### Instructions

1. Spoon the yogurt into a bowl.
2. Top with granola and mixed berries.
