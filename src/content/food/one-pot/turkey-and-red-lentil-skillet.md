### Ingredients

- 28 oz (793g) 93/7 ground turkey (Butterball)
- 1.5 cups dry red lentils (288g), rinsed
- 4 cups Kirkland organic chicken bone broth (plus water as needed)
- 1.5 lb gold potatoes (680g), diced medium
- 2 medium carrots (150g), diced
- 1 red capsicum (150g), diced
- 1 large yellow onion (200g), diced
- 4 cloves garlic (12g), minced
- Cooking spray
- 2 tsp cumin
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1.25 tsp salt (adjust to broth)
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

### Instructions

1. Coat a large Dutch oven or deep skillet with cooking spray and heat over medium-high. Add onion, carrot, and potato; sauté 7–8 min until onion softens and potatoes start to color.
2. Add garlic, cumin, and smoked paprika; stir 30 seconds until fragrant.
3. Push veg aside, add ground turkey. Break up and brown 6–8 min until no longer pink. It will render enough fat to brown without added oil.
4. Stir in red lentils, bone broth, salt, and pepper. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer.
5. Cover and simmer 22–25 min, stirring occasionally, until lentils and potatoes are tender and the sauce thickens. If it gets too thick before everything is tender, add a splash of water. Add capsicum in the last 8 min so it keeps some bite.
6. Taste, adjust salt. Rest 5 min, then portion into 5 containers.
