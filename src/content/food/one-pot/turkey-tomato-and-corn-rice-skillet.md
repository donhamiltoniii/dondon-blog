### Ingredients

- 1.75 lb ground turkey (93/7)
- 1.25 cups jasmine rice, dry
- 2.1 cups (16.9 oz) Kettle & Fire beef bone broth
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes, undrained
- 1 can (15 oz) corn, drained
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 3–4 cloves garlic, minced
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Olive oil for the pan

### Instructions

1. Heat a splash of olive oil in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high. Add onion and cook 3–4 minutes until softened. Add garlic and cook 1 minute until fragrant.
2. Add ground turkey. Break it up and brown until no pink remains, about 6–8 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
3. Stir in the dry jasmine rice and toast 1 minute, coating the grains.
4. Add diced tomatoes (with juice), drained corn, and bone broth. Stir and bring to a simmer.
5. Cover, reduce heat to low, and cook 15–18 minutes until rice is tender and liquid is absorbed.
6. Remove from heat and rest, covered, 5 minutes. Fluff and serve.
