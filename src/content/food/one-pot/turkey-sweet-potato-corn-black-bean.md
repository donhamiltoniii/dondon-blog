### Ingredients

- 2 lb (907g) ground turkey, 93/7
- 600g sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed
- 1 can (15.25oz) corn, drained
- 1 can (14.5oz) diced tomatoes, undrained
- 1 cup black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 140g baby spinach
- 1.5 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1.5 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

### Instructions

1. Brown ground turkey in a large Dutch oven over medium-high, breaking it up, 6–8 min. Season with half the cumin, smoked paprika, chili powder, salt, and pepper. Remove to a plate, leaving rendered fat in the pot.
2. Cook onion 3–4 min until soft (use a splash of broth if it sticks). Add garlic and remaining spices; cook 1 min until fragrant.
3. Add sweet potatoes, diced tomatoes, and broth. Cover and simmer 15 min until sweet potatoes are fork-tender.
4. Stir in corn, black beans, and the browned turkey. Simmer uncovered 5 min to heat through and reduce. Fold in spinach until wilted. Taste and adjust salt.
