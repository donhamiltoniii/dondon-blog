### Ingredients

- 1.75 lb (794 g) 93/7 ground turkey
- 1 cup (192 g) green lentils, rinsed
- 1 yellow onion (~150 g), diced
- 4 garlic cloves (~12 g), minced
- 3 carrots (~180 g), diced
- 5 cups (1180 ml) Kirkland organic chicken bone broth
- 2 tbsp (32 g) tomato paste
- 1 cup (120 g) canned green beans, drained
- 1 tbsp (14 g) olive oil
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

### Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium. Add onion, carrots, and garlic; cook until softened, ~5 min.
2. Add turkey, breaking it up. Cook until no longer pink, ~7 min. Stir in tomato paste and cook 1 min.
3. Add lentils, broth, thyme, salt, and pepper. Bring to a boil, then reduce, cover, and simmer until lentils are tender, ~30 min.
4. Stir in green beans and heat through, ~3 min. Adjust seasoning.
