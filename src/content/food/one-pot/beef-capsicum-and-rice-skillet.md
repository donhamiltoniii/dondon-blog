### Ingredients

- 2 lb (908g) 85/15 ground beef (Costco frozen pack)
- 3 bell peppers (capsicum), sliced
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 can (14.5oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 cup jasmine rice, dry
- 2 cups bone broth
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp salt (to taste)

### Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large deep skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high. Add the onion and cook until softened, ~4 min. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
2. Add the ground beef, breaking it apart. Cook until browned, ~7 min. Drain excess fat here if trimming calories.
3. Stir in the cumin, chili powder, smoked paprika, and salt. Toss to coat the beef.
4. Add the sliced capsicum and cook 2–3 min until they start to soften.
5. Stir in the diced tomatoes (with juice), dry jasmine rice, and bone broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce to low, cover, and simmer 18–20 min until the rice is tender and most of the liquid is absorbed. Stir once or twice to prevent sticking.
6. Turn off the heat and let it sit covered for 5 min. Fluff and serve.
