### Ingredients

- 1.75 lb (794 g) lean ground turkey (93/7)
- 150 g green lentils, dry
- 1 can (15 oz / ~250 g drained) black beans, drained & rinsed
- 1 can (14.5 oz / ~410 g) diced tomatoes, undrained
- 150 g canned corn, drained
- 150 g canned green beans, drained
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 carton (16.9 oz / 500 ml) beef bone broth
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp salt (to taste)

### Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium-high. Add onion and garlic; cook 5 min until softened.
2. Add ground turkey and break apart. Cook ~7 min until no longer pink, then stir in cumin, smoked paprika, and salt.
3. Stir in green lentils, diced tomatoes (with liquid), and beef bone broth. Bring to a boil.
4. Cover, reduce to low, and simmer ~25–30 min until lentils are tender. Stir occasionally; add a splash of water if it dries out before they're done.
5. Fold in black beans, corn, and green beans. Heat through, then divide into 5 meal-prep containers.
