## Ingredients

- 1.75 lb (794 g) lean ground turkey (93/7)
- 100 g jasmine rice, dry
- 150 g red lentils, dry
- 1 can (15 oz / ~250 g drained) black beans, drained & rinsed
- 1 can (14.5 oz / ~410 g) diced tomatoes, undrained
- 150 g canned corn, drained
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 medium bell peppers, diced
- 1 carton (16.9 oz / 500 ml) beef bone broth
- ~200 ml water
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp salt (to taste)

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium-high. Add onion, bell peppers, and garlic; cook 5 min until softened.
2. Add ground turkey and break apart. Cook ~7 min until no longer pink, then stir in cumin, smoked paprika, and salt.
3. Stir in jasmine rice, red lentils, diced tomatoes (with liquid), beef bone broth, and water. Bring to a boil.
4. Cover, reduce to low, and simmer ~20 min until rice and lentils are tender and liquid is absorbed. Stir occasionally so lentils don't stick.
5. Fold in black beans and corn. Heat through, then divide into 5 meal-prep containers.
