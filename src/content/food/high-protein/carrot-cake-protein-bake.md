### Ingredients

**Cake**

- 170g oats
- 95g protein powder
- 120g carrots, grated
- 125g egg whites
- Flavour drops or stevia
- Almond or vanilla essence
- 125ml almond milk
- 200g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 2 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp nutmeg
- 1 tsp baking powder
- Dash bicarbonate of soda

**Frosting**

- 100g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 100g lighter Philadelphia cream cheese
- Vanilla essence

### Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 180°C.
2. Add the oats, protein powder, cinnamon, nutmeg, baking powder and bicarbonate of soda to a bowl.
3. Mix in the grated carrots, egg whites, almond milk, Greek yoghurt and flavouring.
4. Mixy mixy until combined.
5. Pour into a lined baking dish or cake tin.
6. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes until cooked through.
7. Mix together the frosting ingredients and spread over once cooled.
8. Optional but elite tier: sprinkle a little sweetener over the top before serving.
