### Ingredients

**For the fries**

- 500g raw potato (cut into thin fry shapes)
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- Salt (season after cooking while hot)
- Cooking spray

**For the beef**

- 300g 93/7 ground beef
- Cooking spray
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- Salt to taste

**For the onions**

- 2 diced shallots (or 1 small brown onion)
- Pinch of salt
- 2 tsp balsamic
- 1 tbsp brown stevia

**Animal style sauce**

- 45g low sugar ketchup
- 120g 0% Greek yogurt
- 30g light mayo
- 1 tsp mustard
- 1 tbsp pickle relish
- 1/2 tsp Worcestershire

**Other**

- 4 slices low fat cheese (or sub 75g low fat cheddar for more protein)

### Instructions

1. Cut potatoes into thin fry shapes, toss with paprika and cooking spray. Air fry at 200°C/400°F until crispy, ~20–25 min, shaking halfway. Season with salt while hot.
2. Mix baking soda into the ground beef before cooking to keep it from drying out. Cook beef in a sprayed pan over medium-high heat, breaking it up, then season with smoked paprika, garlic powder, and salt.
3. For the onions, sauté diced shallots with a pinch of salt until soft, add balsamic and brown stevia, and cook down until caramelized.
4. Whisk together all animal style sauce ingredients until smooth.
5. Plate the fries, top with beef, lay cheese slices over the hot beef to melt, add caramelized onions, and drizzle with animal style sauce. Serve fresh.
