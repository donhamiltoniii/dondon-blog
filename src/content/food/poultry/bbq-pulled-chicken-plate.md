### Ingredients

- 2.6 lb boneless skinless chicken breast
- 1 box (32 oz) Kirkland chicken bone broth
- 1 bottle (~18.5 oz) Sweet Baby Ray's No Sugar Added BBQ sauce
- 750g sweet potatoes (150g raw per serving), cut into cubes
- 5 cups broccoli florets
- 1 jar pickled veggie slaw (10 servings, 10 cal each)
- Avocado oil spray, salt, pepper to taste

### Instructions

1. Poach chicken breast in bone broth over medium heat until cooked through (~20-25 min, 165°F internal).
2. Remove chicken, pull with two forks. Reduce remaining broth as desired.
3. Return pulled chicken to pot, stir in full bottle of BBQ sauce, simmer to coat and thicken.
4. Toss sweet potato cubes and broccoli with avocado oil spray, salt, and pepper. Roast at 425°F for ~25-30 min, flipping once.
5. Plate 1/5 of the chicken with roasted sweet potato, broccoli, and a scoop of veggie slaw on top.
