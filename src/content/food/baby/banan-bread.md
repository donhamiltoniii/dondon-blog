### Ingredients

- 3 very ripe bananas
- 2 eggs
- 1/3 cup whole-milk plain Greek yogurt
- 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted (or neutral oil)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 1/2 cups whole wheat flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- Pinch of salt

### Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line or grease a loaf pan.
2. Mash the bananas until smooth.
3. Whisk in eggs, yogurt, melted butter, and vanilla.
4. Add flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt. Stir until just combined — don't overmix.
5. Pour into the loaf pan.
6. Bake 40 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean.
7. Cool fully before slicing.
