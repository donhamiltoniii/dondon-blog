### Ingredients

**Mini meatballs (turkey or beef)**

- 1 lb (454g) ground beef 90/10 or ground turkey
- 1/2 cup (30g) soft breadcrumbs
- 1/4 cup (60ml) whole milk
- 1/2 cup (65g) finely grated carrot or zucchini
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp onion powder

**Red lentil veggie stew**

- 1 cup (200g) dried red lentils, rinsed
- 3 cups (720ml) low-sodium broth or water
- 1/2 cup (65g) diced carrot
- 1/2 cup (75g) diced sweet potato
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- pinch cumin

**Salmon or chicken rice bowls**

- 2 salmon fillets (~10 oz / 285g) or 2 boneless chicken thighs
- 1 cup (185g) dry rice
- 2 cups (200g) soft-steamed mixed veg (peas, carrot, broccoli)

### Instructions

1. **Meatballs:** Mix all meatball ingredients and roll into small (~1 tbsp) balls. Bake at 400°F for 15–18 min until cooked through. Cool, then freeze in portions.
2. **Stew:** Simmer lentils, broth, carrot, sweet potato, oil, and cumin about 20 min until soft. Mash or blend partway for texture. Cool and portion.
3. **Bowls:** Cook rice. Bake or pan-cook salmon or chicken until done, then flake into small pieces. Steam veg soft. Store components separately and combine at serving.
4. Reheat any component with a splash of water or milk. Serve warm, not hot, and cut small.

### Macros

_Whole batch ≈ 8 toddler portions. Per portion: ~230 cal, 17g protein, 9g fat, 20g carbs, 3g fiber._

Approx per component (whole dish):

- Meatballs — ~1050 cal · 90p · 60f · 20c
- Lentil stew — ~950 cal · 55p · 18f · 145c · 30 fiber
- Salmon rice bowls — ~1100 cal · 75p · 40f · 120c
