### Ingredients

**Cottage cheese fruit cups**

- 2 cups (450g) whole-milk cottage cheese
- 1 cup soft fruit (mashed berries, peach, or banana)

**Yogurt oat tubs**

- 2 cups (450g) whole-milk plain yogurt
- 1/2 cup (40g) quick oats
- 1 cup smashed fruit

**Bean and cheese roll-ups**

- 1 cup (170g) black beans, mashed
- 1/2 cup (55g) shredded cheese
- 3 soft small tortillas

**Toddler pasta salad**

- 6 oz (170g) small pasta, cooked soft
- 1/2 cup (60g) peas
- 1/2 cup (55g) shredded cheese
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/4 cup tiny-diced soft veg

### Instructions

1. **Fruit cups:** Stir mashed fruit into cottage cheese and portion into small containers.
2. **Yogurt tubs:** Mix yogurt, oats, and smashed fruit, then let sit overnight so the oats soften. Portion.
3. **Roll-ups:** Warm tortillas slightly, spread with mashed beans and cheese, roll, and cut into rounds. Serve cold.
4. **Pasta salad:** Toss cooked pasta with oil, peas, cheese, and soft veg. Serve cold.

### Macros

_Per portion (mixed): ~190 cal, 11g protein, 8g fat, 18g carbs, 2g fiber._
