### Ingredients

#### Chicken

- 5.5 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 3 cups plain nonfat Greek yogurt
- 12 cloves garlic, grated
- Juice + zest of 3 lemons
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp salt
- 1 tbsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp paprika
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 1½ tsp cayenne (optional)
- Spray oil, for the skillet

#### Sweet Potato

- 5.5 lb sweet potatoes, cut into ~20g hunks
- Spray oil
- Season salt, to taste

#### Veg

- 2 lb onion, chopped
- 2 lb carrots, chopped
- 6 oz jalapeño, sliced
- Spray oil
- Season salt, to taste

---

### Instructions

1. **Marinate the chicken.** Whisk together the yogurt, garlic, lemon juice and zest, olive oil, salt, pepper, paprika, cumin, and cayenne. Coat the thighs thoroughly. Cover and refrigerate 2–24 hours.
2. **Roast the sweet potatoes.** Preheat oven to 425°F. Cut sweet potatoes into roughly 20g hunks, spread on sheet pans in a single layer, hit with spray oil and season salt. Roast 30–35 minutes, flipping once, until edges caramelize and centers are tender.
3. **Roast the veg.** On separate sheet pans, spread the onion, carrot, and jalapeño. Spray oil and season salt. Roast at 425°F for 30–35 minutes, stirring once, until softened and browned at the edges. This cooks down hard — expect roughly 60% yield.
4. **Cook the chicken.** Wipe excess marinade off each thigh so it chars instead of steams. Heat a skillet over medium-high with spray oil. Cook in batches without crowding, about 5–6 minutes per side, until 165°F internal. Rest, then slice or leave whole.
5. **Store as components.** Cool everything, then store the chicken, sweet potato, and veg in three separate containers. Keeps 4–5 days.
6. **Build a plate.** 175g chicken + 200g sweet potato + 150g veg. Add fat at assembly if you want it — avocado, tahini, olive oil, or a yogurt sauce.
