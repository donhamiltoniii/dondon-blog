### Ingredients

- Ground turkey, 93% lean, raw (907g)
- Coleslaw mix (794g)
- Dried green or brown lentils (200g)
- Garlic (15g)
- Fresh ginger (15g)
- Scallions (50g)
- Low-sodium soy sauce (3 tbsp)
- Toasted sesame oil (1 tbsp)
- Sriracha (2 tbsp)
- Lime juice (1 lime)

### Instructions

1. Rinse the lentils and simmer in 3 cups of water for 20-25 minutes until tender but still holding their shape, then drain.
2. While the lentils cook, brown the turkey in a large pan over medium-high heat, breaking it up, for 6-8 minutes until cooked through.
3. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Add the coleslaw mix and soy sauce and cook for 4-5 minutes, just until the cabbage wilts but still has some bite.
5. Stir in the lentils and cook for 2 minutes until hot.
6. Off the heat, stir in the sriracha, sesame oil, lime juice, and scallions.
7. Divide into 5 containers and refrigerate. Reheat for 90 seconds in the microwave.
