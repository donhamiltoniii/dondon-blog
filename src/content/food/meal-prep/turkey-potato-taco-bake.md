### Ingredients

- 3 lbs (1360g) ground turkey (93/7)
- 1.5 lbs (680g) russet potatoes, diced ~1/2"
- 1 can (15oz) black beans, drained & rinsed
- 1 can (15oz) corn, drained
- 1 can (14.5oz) diced tomatoes, with juice
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil (1 tbsp potatoes, 1 tbsp skillet)
- 2 tbsp cumin
- 2 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 1.5 tsp salt (plus more to taste)

### Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
2. Toss diced russets with 1 tbsp olive oil and a pinch of salt. Spread on a sheet pan and roast ~30 min, flipping once, until crisp and golden.
3. Meanwhile, heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Sauté onion until soft (~5 min), then add garlic and cook 1 min more.
4. Add ground turkey. Break apart and brown fully, ~8–10 min. Season with cumin, chili powder, paprika, and salt.
5. Stir in black beans, corn, and diced tomatoes with juice. Simmer 5 min to combine.
6. Fold roasted potatoes into the turkey mixture. Transfer everything to a baking dish and bake 15 min.
7. Cool, then portion into 5 containers.
