## Meal prep for today:

### Turkey Lentil Sweet Potato Chili

---

Serves 5

### Macros

| Per serving | Whole pot    |
| ----------- | ------------ |
| ~590 cal    | ~2,950 cal   |
| 43g protein | 214g protein |
| 66g carbs   | 331g carbs   |
| 17g fat     | 87g fat      |

### Ingredients

- 600g sweet potato, cubed
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1.75 lb 93/7 ground turkey
- 1 cup diced tomatoes (with juice)
- 1 cup garbanzo beans, drained
- 1 cup corn, drained
- 1 cup dry red lentils
- 4 cups bone broth
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp salt

### Steps

1. **Roast sweet potato** — Toss sweet potato with olive oil and a pinch of salt. Roast at 425°F for ~25 min until tender and caramelized. Set aside.
2. **Sauté aromatics** — In a large pot over medium heat, sauté onion until soft, ~5 min. Add garlic and cook 1 min until fragrant.
3. **Brown the turkey** — Add ground turkey, breaking it up as it cooks. Brown until cooked through, ~7 min.
4. **Build the pot** — Stir in diced tomatoes with juice, garbanzos, corn, red lentils, bone broth, cumin, chili powder, smoked paprika, and salt.
5. **Simmer** — Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat and cook ~20–25 min, stirring occasionally, until the red lentils break down and thicken. Add more broth if it gets too tight.
6. **Finish** — Fold in the roasted sweet potato. Simmer 5 more min. Taste and adjust salt aggressively — the beans, lentils, and potato mute it.

### Notes

Red lentils dissolve into the base and thicken fast, giving this a stew/chili texture. Turkey macros are raw weight — it cooks down ~25% but the numbers hold. Bone broth adds a little protein depending on brand (~5–10g per pot) not counted above.
