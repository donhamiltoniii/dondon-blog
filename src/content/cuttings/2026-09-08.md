## Overnight Oats Dessert Recipe

## Ingredients

- 1363g Kirkland nonfat plain Greek yogurt (~273g per portion)
- 218g One Degree Organic sprouted rolled oats (~44g per portion)
- 109g Simple Truth PB powder (~22g per portion)
- 670g blueberries (~134g per portion)

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl or container, combine the Greek yogurt, sprouted oats, and PB powder. Stir until fully incorporated and no dry pockets remain.
2. Fold in the blueberries (or reserve to top per-serving if you want them firm).
3. Divide evenly into 5 containers (~536g each).
4. Cover and refrigerate overnight (or at least 4 hours) so the oats hydrate and the mix thickens.
5. Eat cold. Optionally stir in cocoa powder or top with dark chocolate for a dessert finish.
