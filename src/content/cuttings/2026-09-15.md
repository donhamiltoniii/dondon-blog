## One-Pot Turkey, Lentil & Quinoa Skillet

servings: 5

A hearty one-pot skillet built on 93/7 turkey, red lentils, and quinoa simmered in bone broth. High protein, high fiber, and it reheats well all week.

### Ingredients

- 2 lb (907g) 93/7 ground turkey
- ~3/4 cup (130g) dry quinoa, rinsed
- ~1/2 cup (110g) dry red lentils, rinsed
- 1 large yellow onion (150g), diced
- 6 cloves garlic (18g), minced
- 1 can (400g) diced tomatoes
- 4 cups (960g) bone broth
- 2 tbsp (32g) tomato paste
- Spray oil, for the pan
- ~2 tbsp (15g) spice blend: cumin, smoked paprika, salt, pepper (chili flakes optional)

### Instructions

1. Coat a large pot or deep skillet with spray oil, heat over medium-high. Add turkey and brown, breaking it up, ~6–8 min. Season with half the spice blend.
2. Push turkey aside, add onion and garlic. Cook 3–4 min until softened.
3. Stir in tomato paste and remaining spices; cook 1 min until fragrant.
4. Add rinsed quinoa, lentils, diced tomatoes, and bone broth. Stir, bring to a boil.
5. Cover, reduce to a simmer, cook 20–25 min until quinoa and lentils are tender and most liquid is absorbed. Stir once or twice to prevent sticking.
6. Taste and adjust salt. Rest 5 min before serving.
